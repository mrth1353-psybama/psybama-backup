# Psybama — Brand Guide Compliance

Any change or new content added to this website (copy, colors, tone, imagery, taglines, structure) must be checked against the two brand guide source files in `brand_guide/`:

- `brand_guide/برند گاید فیضی.pdf` — official Brand Guide v1.0 (mission, vision, positioning, color palette, typography, brand voice Do's/Don'ts, verbal identity, personas, photography brief, applications)
- `brand_guide/برند گاید تولید محتوا.docx` (plain text also available at `brand_guide/برند-گاید-تولید-محتوا.txt`) — content production guide (elevator pitches, brand story, channel-by-channel tone table, banned words, before/after examples, persona pain points)

Before writing or editing any user-facing text, color, or visual element on the site, verify it against these files. If a proposed change isn't clearly covered by either file, flag the gap explicitly rather than guessing.
